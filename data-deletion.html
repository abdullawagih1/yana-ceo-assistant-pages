User Data Deletion Instructions

How to request deletion of eligible information associated with YANA CEO Assistant.

Last updated October 5, 2026

Requesting Deletion of Your YANA Data

Users may request deletion of eligible personal information associated with their use of YANA CEO Assistant.

How to Submit a Request

Email info@rubix.sa with the subject “YANA CEO Assistant — Data Deletion Request”. Provide enough information to identify the relevant YANA account or communication identity, such as your name, organization, account email and relevant communication channel where applicable.

Security: Do not send passwords, access tokens, API keys, private keys, authentication codes or other security credentials.

Identity Verification

Rubix may verify your identity and relationship to the relevant YANA account or organization before processing a request.

What May Be Deleted

Eligible information may include personal information associated with YANA conversations and requests, account or channel identifiers, mission or task information, connected-service references, calendar-related information processed by YANA, approval and execution-related information, and other eligible personal information maintained by YANA.

Information That May Need to Be Retained

Rubix may retain limited information where necessary for applicable law, security and abuse prevention, audit and accountability, dispute resolution, incident investigation or legal claims.

Processing Time

Rubix will process valid deletion requests without undue delay and within the timeframe required by applicable law. Where applicable law permits additional time because of complexity or request volume, the requester may be informed accordingly.

Connected Third-Party Services

Deleting information from YANA does not necessarily delete information independently maintained by third-party services such as Meta/WhatsApp, Microsoft, or an organization’s own systems. Separate requests may be required.

Confirmation

Where appropriate and permitted, Rubix will confirm completion or explain information that must be retained and the applicable reason.
