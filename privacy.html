Privacy Policy

How YANA CEO Assistant processes, uses and protects information.

Effective October 5, 2026 · Last updated October 5, 2026

1. Introduction

YANA CEO Assistant (“YANA” or the “Service”) is an AI-powered executive assistant developed and operated by Rubix Information Technology Company (“Rubix”), a Saudi limited liability company with Unified National Number 7033430047, headquartered in Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

This Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, shared, protected and deleted when authorized users interact with YANA. YANA is intended for authorized business and executive use and is not directed to children.

2. Information We Process

Depending on enabled features and authorized integrations, YANA may process identity and account identifiers; messages, commands and communication metadata; calendar and scheduling information; requests, missions, actions, approvals, authorization decisions and verification results; technical, security and audit information; and information required to interact with authorized connected services.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to understand and respond to requests, provide AI-assisted functionality, maintain conversation and mission state, perform authorized actions, support scheduling, manage approvals and authorization, verify outcomes, maintain security and auditability, troubleshoot incidents, improve reliability and comply with applicable obligations.

4. AI-Assisted Processing and Automated Actions

AI-generated interpretations or responses do not, by themselves, grant authority to perform an action. Where applicable, approval, authorization, execution and verification are separate controls. YANA may decline, pause, request clarification or approval, or report that an outcome could not be verified.

5. Connected Services and Service Providers

YANA may interact with approved providers and systems including Meta/WhatsApp, Microsoft Graph and Microsoft 365 services, Microsoft Azure, and other systems specifically authorized for a YANA deployment. Third-party services may also apply their own terms and privacy practices.

6. Data Security

Rubix applies technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information processed by YANA, including appropriate access, identity, authorization, credential, audit and verification controls. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Rubix retains personal and operational information only for as long as reasonably necessary for service operation, security, auditability, dispute handling and applicable legal obligations. Different categories may have different retention periods. Information no longer required may be deleted or anonymized under applicable procedures.

8. Privacy Rights and Deletion

Subject to applicable law and relevant exceptions, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with YANA. Identity verification may be required. Certain records may be retained for legal, security, misuse-prevention, dispute, audit or legal-claim purposes. See the Data Deletion Instructions.

9. International Processing

YANA relies on technology providers that may operate infrastructure in multiple jurisdictions. Cross-border processing will be handled subject to applicable data-protection requirements and appropriate safeguards.

10. Saudi Data Protection

Where applicable, Rubix processes personal data in accordance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), its applicable regulations, and other data-protection requirements that may apply.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to YANA, its integrations, applicable requirements or data-handling practices. The current version will be published here with an updated date.
